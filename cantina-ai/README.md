# Cantina AI Video

Shared in [The_MissAI's X post](https://x.com/The_MissAI/status/2125281919952687176), whose video promotes Cantina for making AI videos. Cantina is a character-centered social AI app for creating characters and making short videos with them; it is available on mobile and has a web video experience in beta.

- Product: [Cantina](https://cantina.com/about)
- Help Center: [Making videos](https://support.cantina.com/hc/en-us/sections/43695350694931-Make-Videos)
- App Store: [Cantina: AI Video & Characters](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cantina-ai-video-characters/id1018368216)

Limit note: the post calls video creation free and unlimited. Cantina's current Help Center says Quick videos have no daily limit, but are short character clips based on pre-generated base videos; HQ videos create new scenes and are limited to 10 per day. Check the current app limits and privacy terms before uploading personal images or likenesses. This is a hosted app, not a GitHub project, so there is no repository to fork.
