# VoiceStudio — Local AI Voice & Audio Studio

Shared in [HazratCrypto's X post](https://x.com/HazratCrypto/status/2122660866327797781), with a [short demo video](https://x.com/HazratCrypto/status/2122660866327797781/video/1).

The post describes VoiceStudio as a locally running, open-source desktop app for voice cloning, video dubbing, and audiobook creation, and attributes support for 646 languages to the project. These are claims from the post/project; language coverage and quality depend on the selected speech engines. The post says its caveat is in the first comment, but that comment was not available in the retrieved source.

- Upstream: [debpalash/VoiceStudio](https://github.com/debpalash/VoiceStudio)
- Your individual fork (already existed): [benyamin-persia/VoiceStudio](https://github.com/benyamin-persia/VoiceStudio)
- Project site: [voicestudio.sh](https://voicestudio.sh/)

Voice cloning should only be done with the voice owner's permission. VoiceStudio's [acceptable-use policy](https://voicestudio.sh/acceptable-use) states that consent is required and prohibits deceptive impersonation and other abusive uses. Follow applicable law and disclose synthetic audio when listeners could otherwise be misled.
